cath lescieur

cath lescieur (méxico) es una artista cuya práctica consiste en habitar la teoría como plataforma para saltar al ensayo híbrido y a la poesía: la política radical, la música, el arte y los fenómenos de la cultura pop desde dentro y no desde la distancia, con objetividad parcial. muchas veces su trabajo existe como publicación, práctica impresa — de la risografía a la autoedición, y recientemente el telar digital (tc2).

CATH LESCIEUR

recipes 000%

i've got, what, 170 recipes. each one indexed, all of them archived on my instagram.

i daydream about the timbre of your voice, the resonance your chest makes when you sip something strong.

you told me you once you were sick of work, of the new and old friends, that you want to pursue a new path, a new journey, a master’s degree.

but you never stopped circling back to that georgia o'keeffe show. and neither did i. it doesn't help that they keep a piece of one of the artists in my school's museum. i go look at it sometimes. not for you. for the proof that an afternoon like that was ever available.

deep down i know these recipes are prayerful — saved in the hope you'll break the silence, or that thing we've been carrying for years, and you finally say i’m coming over, but it hasn't materialized yet.

are you still eating frozen watermelon with a spoon, or are you trying new fruits? it’s probably the latter, isn’t it? there's something crude about being this proximate — same rain, same time and place, same stars.

have you muted my stories? have you blocked my number? have you lost the courage to come and finally tell me i’m sorry ha, like if you ever were in your lifetime.

it’s always the damn same don’t ask why i can’t believe you when you — well, would you believe yourself if you were me?

cruising as a desire kept in deferral,

because deferral is how a thing stays possible.

a cruising, crushing desire.

the only evidence i've managed to preserve that you still occupy me — is that every future with you stays not-yet, which is to say stays.

a gaze that refuses to resolve. and a bet placed on the possible. uncertainty not the defect but the whole condition.

i know exactly what i want. that's not the same as believing it will come. it isn't a failure of wanting.

to archive 170 recipes with this kind of devotion is to keep insisting that an afternoon i have never had is owed to me anyway.

i cruise, and the risk is that no one comes. and i search… —cortázar, badly— knowing the finding was never the point.

still trying to find a new fruit you could eat and the honey from it would make your fingers sticky

a new vegan restaurant you would love and you would pretend that is the best day in forever.

a new pretext to write your name into my contacts and finally ask how the hell you've been. or finally say i've missed you terribly.
